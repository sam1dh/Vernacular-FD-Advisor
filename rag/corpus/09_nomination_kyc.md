## Nomination: name a nominee

Banks strongly recommend (and RBI requires banks to offer and record) nomination on every deposit account. A nominee is the person who receives the deposit money if the depositor dies. You can nominate one person per FD, and change the nominee any time by written request. In Hindi: "nominee wo insaan hai jo FD ka paisa lega agar aap na ho".

## Nomination is not a will substitute

A nominee holds the money on behalf of the legal heirs; nomination does not override succession law, but it makes the payout fast and avoids the bank waiting for a succession certificate for small amounts. Keeping nomination updated on every FD is the single easiest step to protect your family's access to the money.

## KYC documents needed

To open an FD you need KYC (Know Your Customer) proof: officially valid documents such as Aadhaar, PAN card, passport, voter ID or driving licence, plus a photograph. PAN is mandatory for deposits of ₹50,000 or more; without PAN, TDS is deducted at 20% instead of 10% on interest.

## Access modes

FDs can be booked at a branch, or online/mobile banking, and many banks now allow video-KYC for digital onboarding. Keep the FD receipt (or e-receipt) safe; it records the rate, tenor, maturity date and maturity amount you were promised.
