## What Section 80TTB gives

Under Section 80TTB of the Income-tax Act, resident senior citizens (aged 60 or above) can deduct up to ₹50,000 of interest income earned from bank deposits — including fixed deposits and savings accounts — from their taxable income each financial year. In Hindi: "60 saal se upar waalo ke FD interest par ₹50,000 tak ki chhoot".

## Only for bank deposits

Section 80TTB covers interest from banks (including cooperative banks and post-office deposits). It does NOT cover interest from company fixed deposits, NBFC deposits or bonds — those do not qualify for the deduction.

## Works with the higher TDS threshold

Senior citizens also get the higher TDS-free threshold on FD interest: from FY 2025-26 banks deduct TDS under Section 194A only when interest to one bank exceeds ₹1,00,000 for a senior citizen (versus ₹50,000 for others). Submitting Form 15H can stop TDS entirely if total income is below the taxable limit.

## Higher basic exemption for seniors

Separately, senior citizens enjoy a higher basic income-tax exemption limit (₹3,00,000 under the old regime). These benefits are distinct: 80TTB is a deduction on interest, the exemption limit decides how much income is tax-free overall.
